Terms of Use

SENEC GmbH continuously seeks to design and develop the SENEC energy storage system to be more cost-efficient for its customers. SENEC GmbH therefore provides its customers with the latest standard version of the storage unit software via updates. To this end, the storage system is connected via the Internet to SENEC GmbH server. The customer's storage system automatically checks at regular intervals whether an update is available and will automatically download and install any updates. An update loses only the day's statistics for the SENEC. No further data loss is incurred.

The updates can involve handling, gathering, processing and use of data (consumption data etc.) which could come under the definition of “personal data” under the provisions of data protection legislation. Furthermore, third parties having a direct relationship with SENEC GmbH can access the data for possible servicing operations becoming necessary. Any other duplication, use or processing is expressly prohibited. The data will only be used for operation and for product improvements. It is forbidden to process protected personal data for a purpose other than the legally authorised tasks without authorisation, to disclose it, make it accessible or use it in any other way. SENEC GmbH undertakes to comply with the provisions of the German Data Protection Law and the German Telemedia Law and to give its staff instructions to this effect and also to train its staff accordingly and to oblige them to comply with the provisions of the German Data Protection Law and the German Telemedia Law.

The customer hereby declares his express consent and conscious agreement to the handling, gathering, processing and use of his personal data (consumption data etc.) by SENEC GmbH for the purpose of improving and optimising SENEC brand energy storage systems, particularly for the achievement of further economies for the customer and to enhance cost-effectiveness.
